WILDLIFE PROTOCOLS

Alligators & Wetland Etiquette

At Everglades Shark Valley, native American alligators sunbathe openly along the edge of the asphalt tram loop:

AMERICAN ALLIGATOR
Freshwater Sloughs
Plentiful along Shark Valley canal. Maintains a calm, non-aggressive posture when respected.
15-FOOT BUFFER RULE
Federal Park Law
Strictly maintain at least 15 ft (4.5 m) distance. Never approach, crowd, or feed wildlife.
  • Trail Mindfulness: While riding the tram or walking near the observation tower, keep hands inside vehicle and stay centered on the pavement.
  • Wading Birds: Never chase roseate spoonbills, wood storks, or great blue herons; observe them using binoculars.
MARINE REEF ETHICS

Reef Sunscreen & Snorkel Stewardship

Dry Tortugas and Biscayne preserve pristine living coral heads that require conscious protection:

MINERAL ZINC ONLY: Chemical sunscreens containing oxybenzone or octinoxate cause coral bleaching and DNA damage. Bring non-nano Zinc Oxide (20%+) reef-safe sunscreen.
  • Never Stand on Coral: Corals are delicate living colonial organisms. Standing on coral breaks decades of growth and kills the colony.
  • No Shiny Jewelry: Remove glittering watches, rings, and chains before snorkeling to avoid attracting predatory barracuda.

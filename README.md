## Route & Ecosystem Highlights
* **The Florida National Parks Trifecta:**
  * **Biscayne National Park:** Dante Fascell Visitor Center at Convoy Point, mangrove boardwalks, and a 3.5-hour boat excursion out to historic Boca Chita Key and living barrier coral reef snorkeling.
  * **Everglades National Park:** Both major districts—Shark Valley on the Tamiami Trail (15-mile guided tram tour & 65-ft spiral observation tower) and the Southern District (Anhinga Trail boardwalk over Taylor Slough & Flamingo Marina on Florida Bay).
  * **Dry Tortugas National Park:** Full-day open-ocean expedition aboard the *Yankee Freedom III* high-speed luxury catamaran 70 miles west of Key West, exploring the massive 19th-century brick citadel of Fort Jefferson, moat seawall walk, and pristine coral snorkeling.
* **The Overseas Highway (US-1):** 113 miles across 42 bridges spanning the Florida Straits, Seven Mile Bridge, Bahia Honda State Park, and hand-feeding 100-lb silver king tarpons at Robbie's Marina of Islamorada.
* **Key West Historic Base:** Victorian Conch architecture in Old Town, Ernest Hemingway Estate (50+ polydactyl cats), and sunset celebration at Mallory Square.
* **Zero Theme Parks:** Completely eliminated Disney and Universal to focus 100% on wild nature, marine sanctuaries, and authentic regional culture.
